# Sponsors

Emojery is free, GPL-3.0, and has no paid tier. Sponsorship pays for the servers that keep public reaction counts online. It buys nothing else: no feature is gated behind it, and no sponsor sees data that other people can't.

Tiers and prices live on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/khasky). This file is the part that outlives them — where a sponsor appears, what I need from you, and what I won't run.

## Where each tier appears

Supporter and Priority supporter carry no placement: the first gets the GitHub sponsor badge, the second gets the queue described below. Everything from README sponsor up is listed here.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| README sponsor | name or logo | — | — | — |
| Bronze | — | name or logo | — | — |
| Silver | logo | logo | — | — |
| Gold | larger logo, above Silver | larger logo, above Silver | thanks in every release you sponsor through | yes |
| Platinum | logo at the top of the section | logo at the top of the section | thanks in every release you sponsor through | yes |
| Diamond | first and largest, above everything | first and largest, above everything | thanks in every release you sponsor through | yes |

Within one tier, placement order is by start date: whoever sponsored first sits first. Every sponsor link carries `rel="sponsored noopener"`, because these placements are paid.

## Sending your logo

Write to <hello@emojery.app> from the account you sponsor with, once the first payment clears, with the display name, the URL to link, and the artwork:

- SVG is preferred. Otherwise PNG at 2x the slot width, transparent background.
- If the mark is unreadable on a dark background, send a light variant too.
- Slot widths at 1x: 120px for README sponsor and Bronze, 160px for Silver, 220px for Gold, 300px for Platinum, 360px for Diamond. Height follows the aspect ratio, capped at half the width.

Artwork is committed to this repository and served from the website's own origin. Emojery embeds no third-party image, script, or tracking pixel on any surface, including for a sponsor — that rule predates sponsorship and doesn't bend for a tier.

## Priority on bug reports and site requests

Priority supporter and above: say so in the issue and name your sponsor handle, or write from the sponsoring account. The issue gets the `sponsor` label and moves to the front of the queue. An issue thread doesn't reveal who sponsors, and GitHub lets a sponsorship stay private — so an unmentioned one gets no priority.

Priority means order, not a deadline. This is a one-person project: a sponsored report is looked at first, never promised a date. Security reports jump the queue for everyone, sponsored or not — see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## What sponsorship doesn't buy

- A vote on the roadmap, on which sites get supported, or on how a reaction count is computed.
- Access to data. Sponsors see the public aggregates everyone sees, and no personal data at any tier.
- Removal or promotion of a comparison, a review, or a mention of a competitor.

Sponsorship is declined or dropped for gambling, adult content, token promotion, surveillance and data-broker services, anything unlawful where the project operates, and any mark that presents itself as part of Emojery. That call is mine and comes with no obligation to justify it; the current period is refunded.

## When a sponsorship ends

A placement is removed within 7 days of the sponsorship ending. Release notes already published keep their thanks — a changelog records what happened rather than who is paying today.
