# Code of Conduct

These are the participation rules for Emojery and how they are enforced. They cover this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, commit comments — and any other space where someone takes part in the project.

## What is expected

Stay civil, stay on the topic of the project, and assume the person on the other side is acting in good faith. Disagree about the code as much as you like; the line is the person.

## What is not acceptable

However good the contribution attached to it:

- personal attacks, insults, or demeaning remarks about someone's identity, background, or beliefs;
- harassment, in the open or in private messages;
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing someone's private information — an address, a legal name, an employer — without their permission;
- deliberate derailing: off-topic political flame, brigading, spam, or reopening a decision that is already settled;
- ignoring maintainer feedback and resubmitting the same contribution;
- anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, reject contributions, and block an account from the repository — at their own discretion and without prior warning. A minor first slip usually gets a warning; harassment does not. Decisions are final within the project.

## Reporting

Report privately to <hello@emojery.app>. A link to the thread and a short description of what happened is enough.

This is a solo project: a report goes to the maintainer and to nobody else, and who sent it stays private. Expect a human reply rather than a committee, and no service-level promise — but every report gets read.

Concerns about the maintainer's own conduct go to [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/) under the [Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies): inside this project there is nobody above the maintainer to appeal to.

A security vulnerability is not a conduct matter — report it as described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
